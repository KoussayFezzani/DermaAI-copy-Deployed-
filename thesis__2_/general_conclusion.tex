\chapter*{GENERAL CONCLUSION}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{GENERAL CONCLUSION}

The DermaAI platform project represents a significant step toward modernizing Tunisian healthcare by leveraging the power of technology to address the sector's challenges. Through the integration of artificial intelligence, web development, and localized conversational agents, the platform empowers users with tools to perform preliminary skin lesion assessments, understand their results through visual explainability, and access continuous medical guidance. The development process, from design to implementation, has been a journey of learning and innovation, resulting in a solution that meets the needs of its target users while prioritizing data security and clinical accuracy.

The evaluation phase confirmed the platform's effectiveness, with strong diagnostic performance metrics, robust API interactions, and a highly user-friendly interface. Despite challenges such as deep learning integration and medical data privacy, the solutions implemented---such as modular architectures and localized offline language models---ensured the platform's success. Looking ahead, the proposed future enhancements, including a mobile app, direct teledermatology integration with clinics, and expanded multilingual support, will further expand the platform's impact, making it a cornerstone of healthcare innovation in Tunisia.

In conclusion, the DermaAI platform project not only fulfills the objectives of our Final Year Project but also contributes to the broader goal of digital transformation in Tunisian healthcare. We hope that this work inspires further advancements in the field, paving the way for a more accessible, efficient, and proactive future for patients across the country.
